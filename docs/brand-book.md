# Skratsch Solutions Brand Book

Version: 0.1  
Owner: Skratsch Solutions  
Primary use: website, proposals, service one-pagers, social profiles, slide decks, signage, and lightweight merchandise

## Brand Idea

Skratsch Solutions helps a business get its technology back into working order.

The brand should feel precise, calm, technical, and practical. It shouldn't feel like a generic MSP, a software startup chasing trends, or a cold enterprise consultancy. The strongest direction is a focused systems mark: a circular control point with routed signal paths.

The idea is simple: bring scattered systems into alignment.

## Personality

Skratsch Solutions should sound and look:

- Clear, not clever.
- Technical, not intimidating.
- Premium, not flashy.
- Direct, not stiff.
- Practical, not generic.

Avoid language that overpromises. Use plain sentences with specific outcomes.

Good:

- Make reporting reliable before adding AI.
- Keep Microsoft 365, endpoints, backups, and support working from the same plan.
- Build the automation when the spreadsheet becomes the bottleneck.

Avoid:

- Transform your business with cutting-edge solutions.
- Unlock innovation through digital excellence.
- Future-ready technology for modern teams.

## Logo System

The logo system uses a circular mark with routing lines. The center point is amber. It represents focus, a clear operating center, and the business outcome. The surrounding ring and routes represent systems, support, data, and software moving through the same plan.

## Logo Usage

Use the primary horizontal lockup on light, off-white, charcoal, or deep navy surfaces.

Minimum clear space is the diameter of the amber center point on all sides. More space is better.

Minimum sizes:

- Horizontal lockup: 180 px wide on screen.
- Standalone mark: 32 px wide on screen.
- App icon: 32 px square on screen.

Do not:

- Stretch the mark.
- Rotate the mark.
- Replace amber with a random accent color.
- Put the logo on busy photography without a solid overlay.
- Add drop shadows to the flat SVG assets.
- Rebuild the wordmark with a different typeface.

## Color Palette

### Color Ratios

Use these ratios as a starting point:

- 50% Ink, Panel, or Paper.
- 25% supporting neutral surfaces.
- 15% Steel or Clay.
- 7% Amber.
- 3% Copper or Signal.

Amber should stay special. If everything is amber, nothing is.

## Typography

Use two families:

- Display: `Space Grotesk`
- Body: `IBM Plex Sans`

Space Grotesk gives the brand its engineered character. IBM Plex Sans keeps service copy readable and serious.

Use `Orbitron` only for the fixed logo wordmark. Don't use it for page headings or body copy.

Recommended CSS:

```css
:root {
  --font-display: "Space Grotesk", Inter, system-ui, sans-serif;
  --font-body: "IBM Plex Sans", Inter, system-ui, sans-serif;
}
```

Use `Space Grotesk` for:

- H1 and H2 headings.
- Navigation.
- Buttons.
- Metrics.
- Short service labels.

Use `IBM Plex Sans` for:

- Paragraphs.
- Service descriptions.
- Blog posts.
- Forms.
- Captions.

Suggested weights:

- Display hero: 700.
- Section heading: 600.
- Navigation: 600.
- Body: 400.
- Body emphasis: 500.
- Small labels: 600.

Do not use all caps for long phrases. It works for labels and metrics. It fails for paragraphs.

## Type Scale

Use a restrained scale. The site should feel sharp, not loud.

| Role | Size | Line height | Family |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hero | `clamp(3rem, 2.25rem + 3vw, 5.25rem)` | `.94` | Space Grotesk |
| Page H1 | `clamp(2.5rem, 2rem + 2vw, 4rem)` | `1` | Space Grotesk |
| Section H2 | `clamp(2rem, 1.45rem + 1.8vw, 2.5rem)` | `1.1` | Space Grotesk |
| Card title | `1.5rem` to `2rem` | `1.15` | Space Grotesk |
| Body | `1rem` to `1.125rem` | `1.65` | IBM Plex Sans |
| Small label | `.75rem` to `.875rem` | `1.2` | Space Grotesk |

## Visual Language

Use routing, alignment, and controlled contrast.

Preferred motifs:

- Circular focus point.
- Thin routed lines.
- Split rings.
- Grid lines used sparingly.
- Warm paper surfaces.
- Dark hardware-like panels.
- Real service imagery with calm overlays.

Avoid:

- Random circuit-board decoration.
- Blue-only tech gradients.
- Purple SaaS gradients.
- Abstract glowing blobs.
- Stock-photo handshakes.
- Generic AI brain imagery.

## Photography

Photography should show work, systems, equipment, and operating context. It should feel useful.

Use:

- Real devices and workspaces.
- Close-ups of infrastructure, dashboards, whiteboards, and collaboration.
- Small-business operations.
- Warm low-contrast light.
- Dark panels paired with amber or paper highlights.

Avoid:

- People pointing at transparent screens.
- Generic data tunnels.
- Overly staged boardroom shots.
- Cybersecurity hooded-person imagery.

## Iconography

Icons should match the logo mark: geometric, routed, and simple.

Rules:

- Use 2 px strokes at 24 px.
- Round caps are acceptable for small UI icons.
- Use amber only for the active point.
- Keep icons legible in one color.
- Prefer direct service metaphors over abstract shapes.

Examples:

- Backup: circular restore route.
- Microsoft 365: connected workspace nodes.
- Data: grouped signal lines feeding one output.
- Support: routed line returning to the center point.

## UI Direction

The website should feel like an operational tool with editorial polish.

Use:

- Dark header and footer.
- Paper content bands.
- Strong contrast between service groups.
- Linked cards with clear focus states.
- Amber for primary action.
- Copper or Signal for secondary categorization.

Do not over-card the layout. Page sections should be full-width bands or clean unframed layouts. Cards are for repeated service items, team entries, and tactical callouts.

## Messaging

Core promise:

Skratsch Solutions brings managed IT, consulting, software, data, and AI into one practical technology plan.

Short positioning:

Managed IT. Clearer path.

Long positioning:

Skratsch Solutions helps small and midsized organizations run, improve, and build the technology their business depends on. We support the systems you already use, modernize what slows you down, and build custom software or automation when off-the-shelf tools stop being enough.

Service pillars:

- Run: managed IT, Microsoft 365, support, security, backups, endpoints.
- Improve: assessments, strategy, business continuity, vendor selection, staff augmentation.
- Build: software development, configuration, integration, automation.
- Understand: reporting, dashboards, data management, AI readiness.

## Voice

Write like an experienced technology advisor who has fixed the problem before.

Use:

- Short, direct sentences.
- Specific systems and outcomes.
- Active verbs.
- Calm confidence.

Avoid:

- Buzzwords.
- Filler claims.
- Fear-based selling.
- Jargon without a business outcome.

Before:

We leverage innovative solutions to empower organizations on their digital transformation journey.

After:

We clean up the systems that slow your team down, then build the tools your business actually needs.
