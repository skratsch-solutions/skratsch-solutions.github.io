## Start with the problem

Tell me what you're trying to fix, improve, automate, or understand. A useful first note usually includes the system involved, who's affected, what you've already tried, and the business deadline driving the work.

You don't need a complete technical spec.

## Common reasons to reach out

- You need dependable IT support for a small or midsized business.
- You want help choosing, configuring, or replacing business software.
- You have data trapped in spreadsheets, legacy tools, or disconnected systems.
- You need a practical plan before a cloud, Microsoft 365, reporting, or custom software project.
