## What the consultation covers

A free consultation is a short working conversation about the business problem behind the technology request. You can use it to sanity-check an idea, frame a project, compare options, or decide whether outside technical help makes sense.

I keep the first conversation practical.

## Bring a few details

- The outcome you want.
- The system, vendor, spreadsheet, database, or workflow involved.
- The people who depend on the result.
- Any deadline, budget range, or constraint I should know about.

## Good fits

Consultations work well for Microsoft 365, cloud migration, reporting dashboards, data cleanup, process automation, software selection, custom software, and small-business IT planning.

If the request needs a specialist outside Skratsch Solutions, I'll say that directly.
